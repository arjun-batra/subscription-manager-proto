| # | Date | Decision | Why | By |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2026-10-25 | Expo SDK 58 in Expo Go, public repo, free Expo account | No Mac build or 7-day expiry, $0, free required checks on a public repo | Arjun |
| 2 | 2026-10-25 | `expo-sqlite` for storage; local `expo-notifications` only, no push token, remote notifications disabled in config | Data stays on the device; no network | Chief |
| 3 | 2026-10-25 | Plain state navigation with three tabs and page-sheet modals, not expo-router | Fewer dependencies for a 3-screen prototype; easy to swap later | Chief |
| 4 | 2026-10-25 | Items with no exchange rate are left out of totals and named on the home screen | Gate 3 default D2; never silently dropped (FR-008) | Chief (Gate 3 default) |
| 5 | 2026-10-25 | The home card leads with "Still due this month" | Gate 3 default D3 | Chief (Gate 3 default) |
| 6 | 2026-10-25 | A subscription stores the charge date you entered as an anchor. All other charges are computed from it, backwards as well as forwards, unless "This is the first charge" is on (stored as a start date) | Keeps the month-end rule exact, counts this month's already-paid charge for subscriptions you already had, and doesn't invent one for new subscriptions (review fix) | Chief |
| 7 | 2026-10-25 | One reminder per subscription (its next charge), for the soonest 60 subscriptions, topped up whenever the app becomes active. This is not "the 60 soonest occurrences": a weekly item's second reminder waits for the next app open | Simplest way to stay within iOS's 64 pending; FR-011 budget minus the expiry slot. README says exactly this. Revisit if Arjun misses reminders | Chief |
| 8 | 2026-10-25 | If a reminder time has already passed (for example, the charge is tomorrow with a 2-day lead), it skips to the following charge rather than firing late | Avoids surprise immediate notifications | Chief |
| 9 | 2026-10-25 | The install-expiry warning (FR-022) is skipped | Expo Go installs don't expire | Arjun (via scope) |
| 10 | 2026-10-25 | CSV dates must be `YYYY-MM-DD`; `;`-separated files are auto-detected | Avoids guessing between US and other date orders | Chief |
| 11 | 2026-10-25 | Reminder ids include the fire time and a hash of the title and body (`renew:<sub>:<date>:<fireAt>:<hash>`), so any edit or settings change replaces the pending notification | Review blocker: id-only diff kept stale reminders (FR-009) | Chief |
| 12 | 2026-10-25 | Changing the home currency clears all rates and says which to re-enter | Review blocker: rates are "1 X = r home" and were silently reused | Chief |
| 13 | 2026-10-25 | The reminder search goes as many charges ahead as the lead time needs, and items whose cycle is shorter than the lead get a warning | Daily items with a 2-day lead got no reminder and no warning | Chief |
| 14 | 2026-10-25 | CSV export adds status, reminders and first charge date columns and guards formula-like cells with `'` | Backups restore cancelled items correctly; blocks spreadsheet formula injection | Chief |
| 15 | 2026-10-25 | All writes use `withExclusiveTransactionAsync`, including category saves (clash check plus upsert) and each schema migration step together with its `user_version` bump. Storage SQL lives in `src/data/sql.ts` and is tested against `node:sqlite`, including a mid-import failure rollback and a migration interrupted before the version bump | Nothing else can interleave with an import; rollback is tested, not assumed | Chief |
| 16 | 2026-10-25 | The FR-019 time-zone test passes the zone explicitly (`toLocalDate(now, zone)`); the app always uses the device zone | Jest workers can't switch the process time zone | Chief |
| 17 | 2026-10-25 | **Out of scope for the prototype:** FR-006 sort and search, per-item reminder lead time (FR-009), and Manage URL validation (FR-014; any URL is opened as typed) | Keeps the week focused; revisit if Arjun asks | Chief |
| 18 | 2026-10-25 | The committed project stays loadable in Expo Go from `npx expo start`: no `runtimeVersion`, no `updates.url`, no `expo-updates` (CI: `npm run check:expo-go`). Primary way onto the phone: `npx expo start --tunnel` (Expo CLI 58 defaults to Expo's tunnel, so ngrok isn't needed). A published EAS Update is a secondary, unconfirmed path, published from a scratch copy | Expo's docs conflict on whether Expo Go loads updates that carry a `runtimeVersion`, and `eas update` always adds one. expo-updates would also add a server check (privacy guard) | Chief |
| 19 | 2026-10-25 | Target SDK 58. The App Store listing shows Expo Go 58.0.5 ("adds support for SDK 58", Oct 22 2026), although Expo's troubleshooting page still says the App Store stops at SDK 54. An SDK 54 fallback is kept on branch `fix/sdk-54` in case the phone can't get Expo Go 58 | Conflicting sources: plan for both. The fallback is a no-PR branch | Chief |
| 20 | 2026-10-25 | CSV import rejects a first charge date later than the next charge date. Export also guards values that already start with `'` in front of a formula character, so they round-trip exactly | Re-review non-blockers N3 | Chief |
| 21 | 2026-10-25 | Free trials and instalment plans added to the prototype; the kill rule is now the four checks (setup, insight, reminder, trust) instead of "opened on 4 of 7 days" | A utility isn't meant to be opened daily | Arjun |
| 22 | 2026-10-25 | A trial is a subscription with a `trialEndDate`. The trial end is the day of the first paid charge, and `amount` is the price after the trial. Nothing is charged before it (it also sets the start date). Free trials only, no paid intro price | Reuses the existing charge maths with one extra field; covers the common "forgot to cancel" case | Chief |
| 23 | 2026-10-25 | Trial reminders fire 3 days and 1 day before the trial end at the reminder hour (fixed, not adjustable). While they're pending they replace the item's renewal reminder for the first charge. They share the 60-reminder window with renewals and plan payments, soonest first | FR-010 and the FR-011 budget, without a new setting | Chief |
| 24 | 2026-10-25 | A trial counts as 0 in the monthly and yearly averages until its end date, then at the post-trial price. In "this month" the first charge counts on its due date. The home row shows a "Trial" badge, "Trial ends in N days" and "Free" | FR-004, FR-007, FR-019 | Chief |
| 25 | 2026-10-25 | An instalment plan is an item with `totalPayments`. Its first payment is stored as the start date, so "N of M paid" is worked out from today's date and needs no manual updating. The edit screen asks for the next payment, payments made so far, and either the number of payments or the last payment date (rounded down to a payment day) | One extra field, nothing to keep in sync by hand | Chief |
| 26 | 2026-10-25 | A payment due today counts as not yet paid (same as "still due"). After the last payment the plan stops producing charges and reminders, drops out of the averages, and moves to "Completed plans". Payments in the month it ends still count in this month's totals | Consistent with FR-019 | Chief |
| 27 | 2026-10-25 | Active plans count in the monthly and yearly averages like subscriptions, and the home card adds "Left on N payment plans". Plans can't have a trial. Each payment gets one reminder (lead time from Settings) saying "payment N of M" and what's left | Keeps it simple; the average is what he pays now | Chief |
| 28 | 2026-10-25 | Schema v3 adds two nullable columns (`trial_end_date`, `total_payments`) in one exclusive transaction together with the `user_version` bump, like v2. Existing rows stay ordinary subscriptions. Tested: v1→v3, v2→v3 with rows, links and settings kept, and a crash before the bump or between the two ALTERs | Crash-safe; existing data survives | Chief |
| 29 | 2026-10-25 | Reminder ids get their own prefixes (`trial:`, `pay:`) next to `renew:`. The diff cancels stale ids with any of the three and leaves the test notification alone | Kind visible in the id; the same diff-based re-sync handles edits and cancellations | Chief |
| 30 | 2026-10-25 | CSV gets two columns, `trial end date` and `total payments`, which round-trip with the formula guard unchanged. A plan's first payment uses `first charge date` (blank means the next charge date is payment 1). Import rejects a next charge date before the trial end and a row with both a trial and a plan | Backups restore trials and plans exactly | Chief |
